About

I think HR should
work smarter.

I work in people operations at a fast-scaling tech company, where I've spent the last year realizing that the most valuable thing I can do isn't execute processes manually — it's build systems that make those processes obsolete.

I started by identifying the parts of my job that were high-friction, high-repetition, and low-creativity. Then I built tools to handle them. The onboarding roundup alone saves me 30+ minutes a day. That time goes back into work that actually requires judgment.

What I'm building toward

A function I think of as HR AI & Automation — a role that sits at the intersection of people operations, systems thinking, and applied AI.

My focus is on architecting our systems foundation. I’m operationalizing our Rippling transition, working with IT to troubleshoot the current setup, while auditing our tech stack to identify high-ROI automation opportunities. In parallel, I’m actively prototyping with the Claude API and Claude Code to evaluate how we can best leverage these tools for our future-state infrastructure.
